# IOSPE 2.1.1.58. Incertum (ownership or divine property)

**Датування.** 0001 … 0001 (роки, від'ємні означають до н. е.)

**Тип пам'ятки.** Base of stemless cup, with fragment of wall and handle.

**Матеріал.** Clay.

**Опис.** Dedication (divine property).

**Зберігання.** Государственный Эрмитаж

**Фізичний опис.** Attic, BG, stemless cup, type: rim offset inside, ca. 450-425 B.C.E. (close to Agora XII, nos. 483-492).

**Письмо.** Graffito. Written clockwise, in a semicircle close to the centre. Alpha with straight crossbar. Four-bar sigma
with splayed bars. The loop of rho touches the vertical at obtuse angle. The use of eta suggests an Ionian speaker.

## Текст

```
Ἀπατόρης
```

## Коментар

There are two views in earlier scholarship about the meaning of this graffito, which is fully preserved - Ἀπατόρης:
either it is seen as a variant of the cultic name of Aphrodite Apatouros of Bosporus who was worshipped at a sanctuary near Phanagoreia.
Aphrodite is attested in Berezan as Syria. Dubois, however, prefers to see here a personal name (IGDOlbia 7) and LGPN lists it as such (V4-15627).
There is another attestation of the name Ἀπατούρη (LGPN V4-15647), at Pantikapaion in the IV cent BCE. The uncertainty of interpretation leads me to identify
the graffito as Incertum.
