# IOSPE 2.1.1.6. Ownership(?) inscription

**Датування.** 0001 … 0001 (роки, від'ємні означають до н. е.)

**Тип пам'ятки.** Foot fragment.

**Матеріал.** Clay.

**Опис.** Ownership(?) inscription.

**Зберігання.** Государственный Эрмитаж

**Фізичний опис.** Attica, BG, band cup, 550-540 B.C.E. (class of Athens 1104), close to gora XII, no. 383-384 (A).

**Письмо.** Graffito. Orthograde.

## Текст

```
Ἀριστ[...]
```

## Коментар

Ἀριστ[- -] on the underside of a foot is likely to be the beginning of a personal name - indicating
an ownership inscription. There would be too little space to include also a deity's name, making it a dedication.
Sigma is four-bar, with very short lower bar; rho with triangular loop, but there seems to have been
an attempt to round it.
