# IOSPE 2.1.1.89. Loomweight with graffito

**Датування.** 0001 … 0001 (роки, від'ємні означають до н. е.)

**Тип пам'ятки.** Loomweight, round.

**Матеріал.** Clay.

**Опис.** Incertum

**Зберігання.** Государственный Эрмитаж

**Фізичний опис.** Ceramic loomweight, V-IV century B.C.E, with two drilled holes close to each other and at one side
of the circumference. Clearly meant for suspension.

**Письмо.** Graffito.

## Текст

```
Ν
```

## Коментар

A discoid loomweight with two suspension holes to one side, with graffito most likely reading N rather than Z.
The meaning of the mark on the loomweight is not clear. If numerical, in the Ionic system N = 50.
