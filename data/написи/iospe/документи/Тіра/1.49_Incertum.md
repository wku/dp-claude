# IOSPE 1.49. Incertum

**Місце походження.** Тира. Tyras.

**Датування.** 0101 … 0300 (роки, від'ємні означають до н. е.)

**Тип пам'ятки.** Panel.

**Матеріал.** Marble, white with grayish tinge.

**Опис.** Incertum.

**Зберігання.** Белгород-Днестровский краеведческий музей

**Фізичний опис.** The front and back are polished, the other sides broken off. On the front - Greek inscription I 46.
The back is reused for Latin inscription. In this secondary use, the marble panel served as panelling for a wall, to which it was attached with cement;
its traces are clearly visible.

**Письмо.** Deeply, evenly and neatly cut letters, adorned with serifs. R with unclosed loop; oval elongated O.
Average distance between lines: 0,7cm.
