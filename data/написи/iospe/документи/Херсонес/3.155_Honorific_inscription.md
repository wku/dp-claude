# IOSPE 3.155. Honorific inscription

**Місце походження.** Херсонес. Chersonesos.

**Датування.** -0014 … -0008 (роки, від'ємні означають до н. е.)

**Тип пам'ятки.** Base.

**Матеріал.** Marble.

**Опис.** Honorific inscription.

**Зберігання.** Неизвестен

**Фізичний опис.** Part of a base, first reused as another base, and then as a threshold for a Medieval sepulchre. The top is carved out;
on the bottom, there are depressions for "the left foot of a statue and for either a spear butt, or some other support" (Latyshev 1899).
On the front: a relief crown and six-line inscription cut following ruled lines.

**Письмо.** Mu with slightly slanting outer hastae, pi with even verticals and extended horizontal,
four-bar sigma with parallel top and bottom bars; absence of decorative elements at the ends of letterstrokes.

## Текст

```
ἐπὶ τᾶς συμμα
χίας γενηθέν
τι τᾶς πρὸς β[α]
σιλέα Πολέμω
να καὶ ἁγεμό
[νεύσαντι].
```
