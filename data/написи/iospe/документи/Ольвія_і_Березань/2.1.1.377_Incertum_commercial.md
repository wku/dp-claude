# IOSPE 2.1.1.377. Incertum (commercial?)

**Датування.** 0001 … 0001 (роки, від'ємні означають до н. е.)

**Тип пам'ятки.** Saltcellar.

**Матеріал.** Clay.

**Опис.** Incertum (commercial?)

**Зберігання.** Государственный Эрмитаж

**Фізичний опис.** Attica, small bowl, ca. 350-325 B.C.E. (close to Agora XII, no. 838 ).
Half of the whole preserved.

**Письмо.** Graffito.

## Текст

```
Λ.
```

## Коментар

Lambda is clear. The second letter or mark is cut off by the break. There is a vertical and a short diagonal issuing for the top of
the vertical, but it seems to turn upward almost immediately creating a squiggle. It might be a sideways sigma, although why it needed to
be so compressed would be unclear.
