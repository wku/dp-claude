# IOSPE 2.1.1.57. Incertum

**Датування.** 0001 … 0001 (роки, від'ємні означають до н. е.)

**Тип пам'ятки.** Fragments of acrocup.

**Матеріал.** Clay.

**Опис.** Incertum

**Зберігання.** Государственный Эрмитаж

**Фізичний опис.** Attica, BG cup (type B, acrocup, ca. 450 B.C.E. (close to Agora XII, no. 443).

**Письмо.** Graffito.

## Текст

```
[- -]Α̣?ΑΦ[- -]
```

## Коментар

The position of graffito below and along the rim is typical for dedicatory or ownership inscriptions. The cup is preserved in fragments, some
of them joining. One fragment carries four letters: alpha, sampi(?), alpha, and phi. On the fragment that adjoins on the left, two small notches
at the break might be endpooints of letter strokes (so represented on the drawing) but could also be accidental flaking due to damage.
