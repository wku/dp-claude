# IOSPE 2.1.1.196. Commercial graffito and production(?) dipinto

**Датування.** 0001 … 0001 (роки, від'ємні означають до н. е.)

**Тип пам'ятки.** Amphora in multiple large fragments.

**Матеріал.** Clay.

**Опис.** Shoulder, exterior. Originally inscribed on complete vessel.

**Зберігання.** Государственный Эрмитаж

**Фізичний опис.** Amphora, Chios, 550-525 B.C.E. (Monachov et al. 2019, p. 73, Ch5: funnel-neck type).
Neck (full height) and part of rim, both handles preserved. Shoulders and belly partly preserved.
Lower part to the foot is also preserved in fragments.

**Письмо.** Graffito.

## Текст

```
ΙΙΡ
```

## Коментар

Small painted brown ring. Noted in Monachov et al. 2019, p. 73, Ch5.
Not mentioned in Monachov et al. 2019, p. 73, Ch5 - apparently, it was noticed.
