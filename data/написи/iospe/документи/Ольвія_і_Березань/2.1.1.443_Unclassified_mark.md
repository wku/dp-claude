## Коментар

I think the strokes point downward relative to the rim and suggest an bisected angle character, or if it was
closed at the bottom (that part is missing) - a bisected equilateral triangle (cf. Johnston 2004, no. 188 on neck of C6 Massaliot(?)
amphora), hence my interpretation as non-alphabetic, but an upside-down psi cannot be excluded.
