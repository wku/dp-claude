## Коментар

A sigma is clear, but there are possible traces of a letter at the break to the left of sigma. If those are in fact traces of a letter,
we may have a horizontal at the top and another at the bottom - consistent with epsilon. If the top trace is accidental or result of damage,
then we my have an omega. The mark could be commercial, but other possibilities cannot be excluded, e.g. ownership graffito.
