# IOSPE 2.1.1.7. Ownership(?) inscription

**Датування.** 0001 … 0001 (роки, від'ємні означають до н. е.)

**Тип пам'ятки.** Fragment of ring foot.

**Матеріал.** Clay.

**Опис.** Borysthenes.

**Зберігання.** Государственный Эрмитаж

**Фізичний опис.** Attica, stemmed dish, 525-500 B.C.E. (close to Athenian Agora XII, no. 974).

**Письмо.** Graffito.

## Текст

```
[...]ραγόρης
Μ̣[- -]
?[- -]
```

## Коментар

One possible personal name that can be restored here is῾Ηραγόρης. It is, in fact, attested several times at Borysthenes: LGPN
V4-18219, VI-V cent BCE (Tolstoi 41 ; = LSAG2 p. 372 no. 58 (vase)); LGPN V4-18221, IV cent BCE (SEG L 702 III, 3);
LGPN V4-18220, IV cent BCE (Dubois, IGDOlbia 106, 7 ; = SEG XLIV 669); LGPN V4-18223, III cent BCE
(Dubois, IGDOlbia 109, 5 ; = SEG XXXVII 673); LGPN V4-18222, IV-III cent BCE (IOlbia 71 II, 4 ; = Dubois, IGDOlbia 11).
Another possible personal name here is Μοιραγόρης, also attested at Borysthenes: LGPN V4-20038, V cent BCE (Dubois, IGDOlbia 101).
We cannot also exclude additional possibilities, not previously attested at Borysthenes, e.g. Ἰητραγόρης, attested at Miletos in the 5th cent. BCE (LGPN
V5b-21606 and V5b-21607); Κυδραγόρης, attested on Thasos, V/IV cent BCE (LGPN V1-37949); Λαμπραγόρης on Chios (LGPN V5a-41774); Μανδραγόρης on Chios, Miletos,
Samos (LGPN V1-48875, V5b-23055, V1-46646); Σφοδραγόρης on Thasos (LGPN V1-39364, V1-37052, V1-37401).
