# IOSPE 2.1.1.159. Production or commercial? dipinto and commercial graffiti

**Датування.** 0001 … 0001 (роки, від'ємні означають до н. е.)

**Тип пам'ятки.** Amphora.

**Матеріал.** Clay.

**Опис.** Borysthenes.

**Зберігання.** Государственный Эрмитаж

**Фізичний опис.** Lesbos, 525-500 B.C.E. (Monakhov et al. 2019, p. 103-104, Hermitage LR.8).

**Письмо.** Dipinto.

## Текст

```
+
Λ
```

## Коментар

A cross-shaped dipinto mark is painted behind the handle, which is a somewhat awkward position.
Jonhston 2004 refers to "lambda-shaped" marks. It is perhaps hard to be certain whether such shapes are lambdas especially
when one of the legs is slightly shorter than the other.
Six short strokes across the length of the handle.
