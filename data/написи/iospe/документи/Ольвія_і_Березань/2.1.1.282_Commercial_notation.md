## Коментар

Numerical notation? Double chi would equal 600+600 in Ionic, and 1000+1000 in Attic systems. This being a cup, the notation
could not be the volume? and it could be the price of a single cup, but perhaps of a consignment of cups or of a whole china set?
All of this would apply if the graffito was made on the complete cup. If made on an ostracon, it could serve as a token or label with
price or other sort of calculation included in a basket or sack with dry goods.
The same mark is listed as trademark in Johnston 1979, type 19B. He says (p. 2) that they are frequent on East Greek vases, but
"it is not necessarily a ligature."
