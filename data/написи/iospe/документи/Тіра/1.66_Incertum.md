# IOSPE 1.66. Incertum

**Місце походження.** Тира. Tyras.

**Датування.** 0001 … 0300 (роки, від'ємні означають до н. е.)

**Тип пам'ятки.** Panel.

**Матеріал.** Limestone, dense, yellowish.

**Опис.** Incertum.

**Зберігання.** Одесский археологический музей

**Фізичний опис.** Bottom left corner of a panel. The front of the epigraphic field is well worked, outside it - treated summarily, the back is also treated summarily. The bottom is
lightly planed. The epigraphic field is inset 0,9cm deep and framed by a wide relief border, which on the bottom runs flush with the edge of the panel and
on the left side if offset by a few sentimeters. The top surface of the relief border is flat, sloping to the inset field at obtuse angle. Border width is 7,0
on the left and bottom.

**Письмо.** Sloppily cut letters, with strokes of varying depth, adorned with serifs.

## Коментар

Справа от сохранившихся букв видна еще одна вертикальная гаста. Первая иота перечеркнута случайным штрихом, не являющемся частью буквы.

## Коментар

To the right of the preserved letters, there is another vertical hasta. The first iota is crossed by an accidental stroke, which is not part of that letter.
