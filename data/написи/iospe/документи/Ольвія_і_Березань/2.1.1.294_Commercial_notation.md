## Коментар

Λ Λ Λ - whether this is an alphabetic mark or a symbol consisting of three anlges is difficult to say, but more likely the latter.
It is unlikely to be an Ionic numeral as lambda stands for 30 and there is a separate mark (koppa) for 90.
