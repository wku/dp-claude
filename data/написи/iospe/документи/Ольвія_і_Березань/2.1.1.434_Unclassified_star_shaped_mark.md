## Коментар

A star-shaped mark (chi with additional vertical through the intersection).
Exactly the same mark is found alone on late VI century BF Attic pots: Johnston 1979, 85, type 32A(i) (addenda: 2006, 62).
Inventory card contains no infomation on the context of find.
Received from the Archaeological Commission in 1904.
