## Коментар

The notches suggest that the object was made as a kind of spool for thread. The graffito on one side consists of a kind of a grid pattern
there is a line that connects notches in a straight line from one side to the other and other lines run roughly parallel to this central horizontal
line while yet other lines cross the horizontal line at different angles.
