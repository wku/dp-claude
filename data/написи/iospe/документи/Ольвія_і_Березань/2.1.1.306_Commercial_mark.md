## Коментар

A shaped sherd with the foot of this stemmed dish fully preserved and the floor of the cup broken all around in a careful
circle slightly smaller in diameter than the foot, with the edges filed off and smooth. Whether the graffito was on the complete vessel (I lean towards this option)
before the broken foot was reused is hard to say.
