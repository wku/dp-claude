# IOSPE 2.1.1.48. Incertum (dedication or ownership?)

**Датування.** 0001 … 0001 (роки, від'ємні означають до н. е.)

**Тип пам'ятки.** Rim fragment.

**Матеріал.** Clay.

**Опис.** Incertum dedication or ownership?)

**Зберігання.** Государственный Эрмитаж

**Фізичний опис.** Attica, BG cup, late VI - early V century B.C.E. (close to Agora XII, nos. 403-413).

**Письмо.** Graffito.

## Текст

```
ΥΡΜ̣ΕΩ
```

## Коментар

Four letters of the graffito, the first two ΥΡ and the last two ΕΩ seem to be quite clear. The third letter is in doubt: if this is a mu, the shape would
be unusual for the Archaic period, with the two middle hastae connecting to the outer hastae below the apices, and the middle hastae approaching each other, but
practically not touching at the bottom. If we read Υρμεω as the Genetive of a personal name,
no such names appear to have been attested. If this is a misspelling or an unusual phonetization, then perhaps Ἑρμέω was intended and we have a dedication.
