# IOSPE 2.1.1.60. Incertum (dedication?)

**Датування.** 0001 … 0001 (роки, від'ємні означають до н. е.)

**Тип пам'ятки.** Rim fragment.

**Матеріал.** Clay.

**Опис.** Incertum (dedication?)

**Зберігання.** Государственный Эрмитаж

**Фізичний опис.** Attica, BG, skyphos, 400-375 B.C.E. (close to Agora XII, no. 349 ).

**Письмо.** Graffito.

## Текст

```
[- -]Ρ̣ΟΙΗ
```

## Коментар

The sequence of letters suggests the ending of a noun in the Nominative, for which there are many possibilities, e.g. civic or religious offices
θεωροί, ἔφοροι, ὑδροφόροι, etc. Omicron is not likely to be representing omega at this date (early 4th cent BCE), but toponyms such as Μεγαροῖ
are also possible.
The graffito is along the rim of a cup, which is often where we find dedications. It would not be a common type, but Nominative forms of divine names are
known on offerings, so [Διόσκου]ροι could be considered. The meaning of the following eta or heta is unclear.
