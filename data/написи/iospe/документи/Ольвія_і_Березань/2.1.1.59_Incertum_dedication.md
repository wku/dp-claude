# IOSPE 2.1.1.59. Incertum (dedication?)

**Датування.** 0001 … 0001 (роки, від'ємні означають до н. е.)

**Тип пам'ятки.** Rim fragment.

**Матеріал.** Clay.

**Опис.** Incertum (dedication?)

**Зберігання.** Государственный Эрмитаж

**Фізичний опис.** Attica, BG cup, V century B.C.E.

**Письмо.** Graffito.

## Текст

```
ΑΧ̣[- -]
```

## Коментар

ΑΧ̣[- -] could be restored as dedication to Achilles (in the Dative or Genitive), or alternatively as a theophoric name, e.g. Achillodoros,
which could be in the Nom. and followed by a deity's name in the Dative. The placement on the lip of
a cup is typical for dedications, but also to sympotic inscriptions. Less likely it is an ownership inscription - these more often appear on underside of
cups.
