# IOSPE 2.1.1.15. Ownership inscription

**Датування.** 0001 … 0001 (роки, від'ємні означають до н. е.)

**Тип пам'ятки.** Pitcher, restored from fragments.

**Матеріал.** Clay.

**Опис.** Ownership inscription.

**Зберігання.** Государственный Эрмитаж

**Фізичний опис.** Pitcher, gray burnish, IV century B.C.E., close in shape to no. 1618 (Agora XII).

**Письмо.** Graffito.

## Текст

```
Πάρμιος
```

## Переклад (en)

[Property] of Parmis.

## Коментар

Πάρμιος is the genetive of Πάρμις, a common name (33 attestations in LGPN online), of which the majority are in the Black Sea
area and Ionia. Parmis is known in Olbia in the III century B.C.E. (LGPN V4-20899: IOSPE I2 201 I, 47 (s. Τιμόθεος)), and attested five times
in Apollonia-Sozopolis in the V-IV centuries B.C.E., as well as twice in Mesambria in the III century B.C.E.; also in Naulochos, Odessos,
and Tomis.
