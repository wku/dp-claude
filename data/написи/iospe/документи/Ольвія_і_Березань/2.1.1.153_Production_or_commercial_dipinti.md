## Коментар

Small ring, empty, painted just below rim and half-way between handles, on a vertical axis.
Small ring, empty, painted on the shoulder, below Text 1, on the same vertical axis. Monakhov et al. 2019, 74, does not mention a circle on the shoulder, but the photo and drawing show them.
Monakhov et al. 2019, 74, says that small rings appear on both sides of the neck.
Monakhov et al. 2019, 74, says that small circles appear on both sides of the neck,
but I presume he also means the shoulder, in similar position (on the oppositde side) to Text 2.
Monakhov et al. 2019, 74, reads ligatured ΑΥ, but I have doubts. Pi, perhaps lambda, and possibly ypsilon. We encounter
monograms with small lambda inside pi (with one short vertical) in later dipinti on amphorae (?) and on Olbian coins
(e.g. Karyshkovsky 2003[1968], 81, Table 4, no. 83).
