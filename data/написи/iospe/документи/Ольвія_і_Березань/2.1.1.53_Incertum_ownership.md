# IOSPE 2.1.1.53. Incertum (ownership?)

**Датування.** 0001 … 0001 (роки, від'ємні означають до н. е.)

**Тип пам'ятки.** Fragment of.

**Матеріал.** Clay.

**Опис.** Incertum

**Зберігання.** Государственный Эрмитаж

**Фізичний опис.** Attica, plate, early V century B.C.E.

**Письмо.** Graffito.

## Текст

```
[- -]Μ̣ΦΟ[- -]
```

## Коментар

The circle with crossbar is a stemless phi rather than a theta, in my view, and the letter before phi, is
either a lambda or a mu: what might look like a crossbar of alpha is likely an accidental stroke, slip of a writing implement. Below that letter
is another slip of the writing tool: the slips, in my view, are indicated by thinner lines than intentional strokes.
Graffiti on the interior of a cup are usually either sympotic or dedications, less typically - ownership.
It is possible to restore a name here, e. g. [Νυ]μ̣φο[δορος], in the Nominative or in the Genitive. Alternatively, [- -]Λ̣ΦΟ[- -] could be read [Δε]λ̣φό[ς]
in the Nominative or in the Genitive - this name is attested in Chersonesos in the IV and III cent B.C.E. (V4-17004, V4-16997, V4-16998),
but in Olbia-Borysthenes only in the II cent C.E.
