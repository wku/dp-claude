# IOSPE 2.1.1.162. Production or commercial dipinto and graffiti marks

**Датування.** 0001 … 0001 (роки, від'ємні означають до н. е.)

**Тип пам'ятки.** Amphora.

**Матеріал.** Clay.

**Опис.** Borysthenes.

**Зберігання.** Государственный Эрмитаж

**Фізичний опис.** Herakleia, late V - early IV century B.C.E., stamp of maker Ramphios (Monakhov et al. 2019, Cat. HP.18).
Almost fully preserved, small fragment of wall missing on one side, tip of base broken off.

**Письмо.** Dipinto in red.

## Текст

```
+
Τ̣ Π̣
```

## Коментар

It is clear that the cross-shaped mark was cut before the red circle was painted over it.
The large red dot (filled circle) painted over Text 1 suggests either an attempt to emphasize (make more visible) or
conversely, to obliterate, cover up the earlier mark.
The two marks with a bit of space between them appear alphabetic (roughly tau or gamma and pi), but if so, they are carelessly
cut, with the horizontal of tau cutting across the top of othe vertical rather than resting on top it; the verticals of pi are not parallel,
and the horizontal extends beyond the verticals to the left and right, while the left vertical extends up beyond the horizontal.
