# IOSPE 2.1.1.29. Incertum (ownership?)

**Датування.** 0001 … 0001 (роки, від'ємні означають до н. е.)

**Тип пам'ятки.** Wall fragment.

**Матеріал.** Clay.

**Опис.** Ownership(?)

**Зберігання.** Государственный Эрмитаж

**Фізичний опис.** Amphora, Ionia (North?), late VII - early VI century B.C.E.

**Письмо.** Graffito.

## Текст

```
ΛΕΟ[...]
```

## Коментар

The direction of writing is retrograde. ΛΕΟ̣[- -] is probably a personal name, such as Leontiskos or Leophron, e.g. attestered in Ephesos in the 4th century BCE.
We would not expect an omicron to be representing omega here, where an Ionian script would be in use both at the likely production site and
at Borysthenes.
